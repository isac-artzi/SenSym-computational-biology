The Simplified Guides

The same theory and math as the weekly chapters, explained more slowly, with more worked examples, analogies and small calculators

Each weekly chapter of this course is written like a university textbook: compact, rigorous, and full of derivations. These companion pages cover the same ideas in a different way: everyday analogies first, every formula taken apart piece by piece, numerical examples worked step by step, a "try it" calculator, and short check-yourself questions with answers.

How to use the two versions together
  1. Read the simplified page for the week first, to get the picture and the vocabulary.
  2. Then read the full chapter. When a derivation gets hard, flip back to the matching example in the simplified page and redo it with your own numbers.
  3. Do the exercises, labs, Python work and check-in from the full chapter. The simplified pages leave things out on purpose.
